Hardware Architecture & LTspice Diagnostics

Gate-Level Array

Constructed via LTspice Special Functions library comprising two NOT inverters (A1, A2) for select complementation, four 3-input AND gates (A3–A6) for decoding address lines, and cascaded OR gates (A7–A9) to form the output bus.

Floating Node & Impedance Resolution

Initial transient runs exhibited nano-volt (`nV`) floating node anomalies (n006). A 10 kΩ pull-down resistor (R1) was connected between node `out` and GND, clamping leakage states and establishing sharp 0V/5V digital levels.

Industrial Context

Reduces pin demand on industrial PLCs/microcontrollers from $N$ channels down to a single analogue/digital telemetry pin plus $\log_2(N)$ binary select lines, preventing cabling weight and cross-talk on factory floors.
